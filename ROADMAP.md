## High Priority

### Complex Insights Dashboard
Give users deep, data-driven insights for each apartment complex. Accessible from the complex detail sheet.

**Potential views:**
- **Peak hours chart** — bar/line graph showing what time of day booter activity is most frequently reported (e.g. "Most reports between 10 PM – 1 AM")
- **Day-of-week breakdown** — which days booters are most active (e.g. weekends vs weekdays)
- **Frequency trend** — rolling 30/60/90-day trend line: is this complex getting more or less active?
- **Average response time** — how quickly after a report does the booter leave (based on follow-up "all clear" reports)
- **Risk score history** — how the complex's risk level has changed over time
- **Comparison view** — "This complex has 3x more reports than the Rexburg average"

**Why it matters:** Turns raw community data into actionable intelligence. Helps students make informed decisions about where to park — and even where to sign a lease.
